Privacy policy

Royal Hot Chicken, LLC · 1903 Binney Drive, Fort Pierce, FL 34949 · store@royalhotchicken.com · Updated November 10, 2026

What we collect

When you join the Supernova Club, we collect your name, email address and mobile number. When you order, our payment system (Clover) processes your card; we don't store full card numbers.

How we use it

To send the texts and emails you signed up for, run your rewards account, fill catering orders.

Who we share it with

Only the services that help us run the business: our point-of-sale and payment processor, our email and text-message providers, and Google (which stores signup forms). We never sell or rent your information, and we never share your mobile number or text-message consent with third parties for their marketing.

Text messages

Message frequency varies, up to 4 messages a month. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to unsubscribe or HELP for help.

Your choices

Unsubscribe from email with the link in any email, or from texts by replying STOP. To see, change or delete your information, email store@royalhotchicken.com.

Our app

The Royal Hot Chicken app shows our menu, takes catering requests and runs your Supernova Club account. If you sign up in the app, we keep your name, email, mobile number and the date you agreed to messages. If you allow notifications, we keep your device's push token so we can send deals and order updates; turn notifications off any time in your phone's settings. Orders are paid through Clover; the app never sees your full card number.

The app does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, shows no ads, and doesn't use your location. We keep basic server logs (like IP address and time) for security and to stop abuse.

Delete your account: in the app, open the Rewards tab and tap Delete account. That removes your details and push token from our system right away. You can also email store@royalhotchicken.com.

Kids

The Supernova Club is for people 13 and older. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13.
