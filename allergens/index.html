Allergen information

We fry everything in refined peanut oil, and Royal Fire Sauce is made with peanut butter. Breaded chicken and fries share the same fryer, so fries may contain wheat and milk.

Our kitchen is small. We can't guarantee any item is free of an allergen. If you have a severe allergy, tell us at the window before you order.
